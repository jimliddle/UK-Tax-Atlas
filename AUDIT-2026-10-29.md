# UK Tax Atlas — October 2026 reconciliation audit

**Audit date:** 29 October 2026. **Baseline:** 204 atlas records; **after reconciliation:** 257 records (53 entries added). **Existing record corrections:** 37. Stable `#item-ID` links were retained for existing entries.

## Why this audit was necessary

The initial Budget 2026 update appended announcements but did not reconcile existing entries with Budget 2025. The cash ISA restriction was left as impending even though it had been cancelled. The savings-income tax change was described as +2 percentage points without explaining the additional +2 points already scheduled. The same issue affected property income tax. These errors have been corrected in place, and a wider review made against the official sources.

### Major corrections

- **#175 Cash ISA restriction:** Budget 2025's proposed under-65 £12,000 cash ISA cap from April 2027 has been **reversed** by Budget 2026. The limit remains **£20,000** for all eligible adults. Historical record retained with status **reversed**.
- **#25 Savings interest tax, #187 Budget savings change:** 2026/27 rates **20%/40%/45%**. Budget 2025 planned **22%/42%/47%** from April 2027; Budget 2026 added **another 2 percentage points**, giving **24%/44%/49%** from 6 April 2027. Four-point cumulative rise.
- **#177 Property income tax:** Same two-stage +4-point change, but the announced **24%/44%/49%** 2027/28 property-rate schedule covers **England, Wales and Northern Ireland**; Scottish powers require separate treatment.
- **#81 Vaping Products Duty:** **already in force from 1 October 2026**, £2.20 per 10ml.
- **#173 Building Safety Levy:** in force for qualifying new English building-control applications from **1 October 2026**.
- **#116 Sustainable Aviation Fuel mandate:** in operation since **1 January 2025**, not a future charge.
- **#119 Carbon Price Support:** still applies as at audit date, but **removal announced for April 2028**.
- **#18 Pension salary sacrifice:** policy for **6 April 2029** taxes annual salary sacrifice above **£2,000** under employee and employer Class 1 NIC.
- **#37/#38 APR/BPR relief:** from 6 April 2026 a combined **£2.5m** 100%-relief allowance, 50% relief on qualifying excess, subject to conditions.
- **#111 EV mileage duty:** scheduled for **April 2028**, **3p/mile** for battery/hydrogen vehicles and **1.5p/mile** for plug-in hybrids; separate from general road pricing.
- **#83/#84 Soft Drinks Levy:** rates uprate April 2027; new 4.5g/100ml threshold and milk-based exemptions removed January 2028.
- **#194 Private-hire operator VAT:** the **£50m operator threshold is a consultation lead option, not a settled limit**.
- **#195 Marketplace VAT:** £90,000 per-platform criterion proposed subject to secondary rules; personal sales, second-hand and B2B excluded.
- **#170 Gas standing charges:** existing charge continues; government has asked Ofgem to abolish domestic gas standing charges by end 2027 if feasible.
- **#30/#31 IHT bands and #59 duplicate Council Tax:** allowances/cross-list are not independent taxes; counting should not interpret atlas record count as tax count.

## Review method and limitations

Reviewed the catalogue's 204 baseline entries by name, classification and policy status; checked major date-sensitive discrepancies and Budget 2026 tax changes against official documentation. Corrected identified errors and added missing household-relevant tax or quasi-tax changes. This was **not** a line-by-line legal verification of every historic enabling-Act hyperlink or each local-authority tariff. Some entries are cross-listed, others describe allowances, reforms, compliance costs, proposed taxes or historical measures: **record count is not a count of unique current taxes**. For applications involving a particular transaction, check commencement and enacted legislation at the time.
